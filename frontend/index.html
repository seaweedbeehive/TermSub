How to use TermSub

1

Upload a video, audio or text file

Upload a video, audio or text file. Choose your source and target languages — or leave Target Language blank if you just want the transcript in the original language, with no translation.

2

Review extracted terms

TermSub identifies key terminology — names, brands, technical terms. Edit them to lock in consistency.

3

Translate & edit subtitles

Get context-aware translations in 59 languages. Edit timing and text of subtitles.

4

Export

Download your subtitles as SRT, VTT, TXT, or JSON.
